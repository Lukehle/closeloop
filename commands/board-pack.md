# /board-pack

$ARGUMENTS

Run the **startup-board-pack** skill, with `saas-metrics` for definitions and `fin-artifact` for
visuals.

1. Confirm every figure ties out to **closed** figures. An open-period extract is provisional and
   must not go in a board pack.
2. Build in arc order: the ask, the scoreboard, cash and runway, growth, what changed, risks,
   functional updates, appendix. Sections 1-3 must stand alone.
3. Scoreboard gets a verdict per line — `On track`, `Watch`, `Behind`, `At risk` — used consistently
   with prior periods.
4. Runway shows both current and planned burn, with a zero-cash date. If runway is under 12 months
   it moves to the ask slide.
5. Frame every benchmark against the company's **stage**. Do not report a metric the data cannot
   support; say "not yet meaningful at n=11" instead.
6. Present any miss number-first, then driver, then recurring-vs-one-time, then what changes, then
   the trigger point.
7. Run the pre-send checklist, including the disclosure checks in `finance-guardrails` Rail 5.
8. Run `finance-quorum` before release.

**Assemble and stage it. A human sends it.**
