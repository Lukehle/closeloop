# /flux

$ARGUMENTS

Run the **flux-analysis** skill.

1. Confirm the accounts are tied out first — do not explain the movement of an unproven number.
2. **State the materiality gates before running them.** Absolute floor, percentage gate,
   zero-tolerance accounts, and the always-investigate list.
3. Pull GL detail for every account over the gate, sorted by absolute amount descending. Three to
   five transactions usually explain a material variance.
4. Decompose each driver — price/volume/mix for revenue, rate/volume/timing for personnel, and
   recurring/one-time/timing/correction for everything.
5. Write commentary with all four parts: magnitude, driver, evidence, forward implication. A comment
   without the forward implication is incomplete.
6. Build a bridge for any material aggregate. It must foot exactly, with no plug.

Reject any commentary that restates the arithmetic, cites "timing differences" without quantifying
them, or explains a variance with "increased business activity".
